🎁 TakeItFree

We share. We connect.

🌍 Project Overview

TakeItFree is a community-driven social sharing platform where people give away items they no longer need — completely free — while building real human connections.

It combines:
	•	Social media experience (feeds, profiles, stories)
	•	Local community sharing
	•	Trust-based identity
	•	Zero-selling culture

Unlike traditional marketplaces, TakeItFree focuses on generosity, storytelling, and meaningful interaction — not transactions.

⸻

🎯 Core Objectives
	•	Encourage reuse and reduce waste
	•	Build trust through verified identities
	•	Make giving social, emotional, and rewarding
	•	Deliver a modern experience similar to Instagram

⸻

👤 User Authentication & Identity

Sign-Up Options
	•	Email providers (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)
	•	Phone number with OTP verification

Identity Setup (Instagram-Style Flow)

After signup, users create:
	•	Unique username (e.g., akhil_shares)
	•	Profile photo
	•	Short bio

Benefits
	•	Real verified users
	•	Strong community trust
	•	Easy social interaction
	•	Prevents anonymous misuse

⸻

🧾 User Profiles

Each profile displays:
	•	Profile photo & username
	•	Bio
	•	Total items shared
	•	Appreciation count (community gratitude)
	•	Active shared items
	•	Past shared history

Purpose: reputation building + community recognition.

⸻

📰 Home Feed (Core Experience)

A scrollable social feed showing shared items.

Each post includes:

📸 Multiple item photos
📝 Personal story behind sharing
🏷 Category (Books, Clothes, Electronics, Furniture, Toys, etc.)
📍 Optional location radius
🤍 Interactions
	•	Appreciate (instead of Like)
	•	Comment
	•	Request Item

⸻

🎠 Rich Post Presentation

Each share can feature:
	•	Item photos
	•	Condition details
	•	Pickup information
	•	Story slide

This creates a modern, engaging content experience — not just listings.

⸻

📩 Organized Request System

Instead of chaotic DMs:
	•	User taps Request Item
	•	Writes a short reason/need
	•	Owner can:
	•	Approve one requester
	•	Decline respectfully

Keeps everything transparent and polite.

⸻

🔍 Discovery & Browsing

Users can explore by:
	•	📍 Nearby items
	•	📦 Category
	•	🔥 Trending shares
	•	🆕 Newly posted

Future expansion: interactive map view.
